I am currently an M.S. student in Computer Technology at the Faculty of Computer Science and Technology, Qilu University of Technology (Shandong Academy of Sciences), under the supervision of Prof. Yongjin Xian. I am a member of the Multimedia Security Computing Group led by Prof. Bin Ma, where my research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence and multimedia forensics, particularly Deepfake detection and robust digital image watermarking.

Previously, I received my Bachelor of Engineering degree in Computer Science and Technology from Qingdao Agricultural University in 2023.
